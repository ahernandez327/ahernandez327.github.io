About

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at Georgia Tech, advised by Dr. Nagi Gebraeel in the Predictive Analytics and Intelligent Systems (PAIS) Lab. My work combines federated learning, distributionally robust optimization, and differential privacy for fault diagnostics in industrial systems.

I am a National GEM Consortium Fellow sponsored by NASA. Before Georgia Tech, I earned my B.S. in Industrial Engineering at the University of Puerto Rico – Mayagüez, and interned at NASA Ames Research Center and Verizon.

Research

Federated Fault Diagnosis with Distributional Robustness and Differential Privacy

With Michael Ibrahim and Nagi Gebraeel · PAIS Lab, Georgia Tech

  • A federated learning framework that lets multiple organizations jointly diagnose equipment faults without sharing their operational data.
  • A distributionally robust multiclass model that stays reliable under uncertainty in both sensor features and fault labels.
  • Differential privacy integrated into training, with formal privacy guarantees for each participant, a new reformulation of the model, and an algorithm to solve it.
